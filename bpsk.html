Aim

To study Binary Phase Shift Keying (BPSK) modulation and demodulation techniques through simulation and analysis.

Objectives:

  • Understand BPSK principles and binary data representation using phase shift.
  • Generate and visualize BPSK signals for various binary inputs.
  • Carry out BPSK demodulation and BER analysis under noisy conditions.
  • Calculate key performance metrics like bandwidth and modulation index.

Theory

Introduction to BPSK

BPSK is a digital modulation technique that encodes data as two phases of a carrier: typically 0° for binary '1' and 180° for binary '0'.

BPSK Mathematical Representation

s(t) = A × cos(2πfc t + φ), where φ = 0 for '1', φ = π for '0'

Parameters

  • Bandwidth: About 2 × bit rate
  • Modulation Index: For BPSK, always 1 (π phase shift)

Demodulation Principle

Demodulation is often done by correlating the received signal with a reference carrier, then making a decision based on the output sign.

Applications

  • Satellite modems
  • RFID systems
  • Wireless communications
