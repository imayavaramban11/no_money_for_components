Aim

To study Amplitude Shift Keying (ASK) modulation and demodulation.

Theory

ASK is a digital modulation technique in which the amplitude of a carrier signal is varied in accordance with the binary data.

Binary 1 → High amplitude

Binary 0 → Low or zero amplitude

Procedure

  1. Enter binary data
  2. Set carrier frequency and amplitudes
  3. Click Generate
  4. Observe modulation and demodulation
